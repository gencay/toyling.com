About the films & places

All 20 X100VI film modes, including ACROS and Monochrome filter variants, are represented by original palette approximations. These are not Fujifilm's proprietary simulations. Film grading also colors the paper and interface.

The cities are endless, reseeded impressions, not navigable maps. Landmarks recur as the camera travels. Munich adds orderly signal phases; London uses left-hand traffic. Motorcycle filtering is a fictional simulation setting, not a claim about local road law.

The blue motorcycle is a tiny top-view interpretation of a 2023 Harley-Davidson Sportster S: fat tires, blue tank, low seat and twin high-side exhausts. All audio is original, synthesized locally. Enable sound with an interaction; pause or mute silences it.

The original generative score uses slow, spacious chords, soft bell plucks and a quiet bass pulse; no game recording or melody is used. Music can be switched off separately. Ambient vehicles keep persistent positions, yield at junctions and brake before water. Filtering motorcycles steer around cars only into clear gaps. Clicked vehicles choose the least risky lane and speed over a four-second forecast, but a crowded insertion or later traffic change can still cause a crash. Only crashes involving player vehicles dispatch a helicopter; nearby traffic queues, not the entire city. Exposed riverbanks have shaded drop-offs; wandering pedestrians can slip over an edge. Water losses get a brief cartoon splash instead of a rescue. Night flight adds illuminated windows, streetlights and headlights without changing the selected film.

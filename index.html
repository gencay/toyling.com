Our first game

Fuji City

A tiny, procedural city movie in an instant-photo frame: moving people, homebound cars and motorcycles, an east-to-west day/night cycle, original synthesized sound, and film-inspired colour treatments.

  • 19 destinations, from Paris and Tokyo to Dublin and Antalya
  • 102 named landmark motifs, drawn as original miniatures
  • Click a road to add a car or motorcycle; rescue helicopters clear the wrecks
  • Every print carries an original, handwritten-style two-line poem

The studio

Characters that adapt. Stories that evolve.

Toyling is an indie studio making mobile and Steam games where AI is part of gameplay, including adaptive characters and evolving stories.

Our mark is a mountain wearing a smile and a game controller. It takes its shape from Mount Ida in Crete, the mythic mountain of Greek legend, and from the snowy peaks of the Alps: a place where stories begin.

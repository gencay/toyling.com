Inkself privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026. Applies to Inkself and the Inkself Keyboard for iPhone.

The short version

Inkself turns your handwriting into a font on your iPhone. Your writing, photos and fonts stay on your iPhone. Inkself has no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What the app keeps, and where

  • Your letters and fonts: the letters you draw, scan or photograph, and the fonts made from them. They are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone, and in your device backups.
  • Photos: the camera is used only when you scan a page or snap a letter. A photo you choose from your library is shared with the app by the system photo picker, one photo at a time. Inkself reads the handwriting on the iPhone; no image leaves it.
  • Free-plan counters: how many free scans and improvements you used, stored on your iPhone (in the app's storage and in the Keychain).

The Inkself Keyboard

  • It types normal text without any special access.
  • With Full Access, which you can turn on in Settings, it reads your chosen font from the Inkself app and copies the handwriting image so you can paste it. Full Access is not used for anything else.
  • It does not keep, log or send what you type, and it does not use the network.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifier.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising networks.
  • The app can read a small settings file from toyling.com that says which offers and features it shows. The request carries no personal data.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. Inkself gets no payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers the payment.

Deleting your data

Delete a font in the app to delete its letters and scanned pages. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept, except the free-plan counters in the Keychain, which hold no personal data.

Children

Inkself is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version collects any data, this policy will say what and why before that version is released, and the date above will change.

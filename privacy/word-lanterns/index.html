Word Lanterns privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026. Applies to Word Lanterns for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the web version on toyling.com.

The short version

Word Lanterns keeps your game on your device. It has no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your progress and settings: rounds and levels, coins, free hints and brushes, badges, your play streak, purchases, sound and text settings, and your language. They are stored only on your device (in the app's own storage, or in your browser's storage on the web).
  • Your profile: the name and companion animal you choose, and a photo if you pick one. The photo is copied into the app's private folder (or your browser's storage) on your device and is never uploaded. You can remove it at any time in your profile.
  • Sign-in buttons: the Facebook and Apple buttons in this version are a preview. They do not connect to Facebook or Apple and send nothing; the game only remembers on your device that you pressed one.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifier.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising networks in this version. The ad spaces you see are placeholders.
  • It does not ask for any permission. Choosing a photo uses your device's own photo picker, which shares only the one photo you choose.
  • Purchases in this version are test purchases. Nothing is charged and no payment data is handled.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress clears your progress, coins and purchases. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data in your browser) deletes everything it kept, including your photo.

Children

Word Lanterns is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

When a future version adds sign-in, purchases through the App Store or Google Play, ads or analytics, this policy will say what each one collects before that version is released, and the date above will change.

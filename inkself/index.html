Inkself: your handwriting, a real font

Scan a page you wrote, snap one letter at a time, or draw on the screen. Inkself makes a font that you install on your iPhone and use in Pages, Keynote, Canva, Procreate and more, and a keyboard that writes in your hand in any app.

Questions

  • Where is my font? After Install, it is in the font list of apps that support installed fonts. Manage it in Settings › General › Fonts.
  • The keyboard types plain text only. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Inkself and turn on Allow Full Access, then choose a font in Inkself and turn on "Use in the Inkself Keyboard".
  • A letter came out wrong. Open the font, tap the letter, then redraw it, retake it with a photo, or rescan the page.
  • Restore purchases. Settings in the app › Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel the yearly plan. Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Inkself.
